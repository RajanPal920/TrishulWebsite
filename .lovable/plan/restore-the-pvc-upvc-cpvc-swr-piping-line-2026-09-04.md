# Restore the PVC / UPVC / CPVC / SWR piping line

Bring back the polymer piping product line, credited to the sister concern **Apex Polymer & Engineering** as the in-house manufacturing source. Structural steel (A36 / A572) and the angle bar section stay removed.

## What the site will show again

- A sixth product category on `/products`: **PVC, UPVC, CPVC & SWR Piping**, with its own category card and standards list (IS 4985, IS 13592, IS 12818, ASTM D1785, ASTM F441).
- Five product pages: UPVC pressure pipes, borewell column pipes, CPVC pipes, SWR drainage pipes, PVC fittings. Product photography for all five is already in the project.
- A sixth material family: **PVC / UPVC / CPVC Piping** with three grade pages — UPVC (PVC-U), CPVC (PVC-C) and SWR PVC — listed on `/materials` and cross-linked through the material × product matrix.
- **Apex attribution** on the PVC category page and each of the five PVC product pages: manufactured in-house by our sister concern Apex Polymer & Engineering, giving direct control of moulds, batch records and lead time. No Apex mention elsewhere on the site.
- Smaller restorations: a PVC pipe filter option in the grade/size finder, a PVC row in the landed-cost estimator, the UPVC density option in the weight calculator, and the "UPVC / CPVC / SWR piping systems" line in the shipping scope list.

## Positioning

PVC stays clearly separated from the CRA metallurgy story — presented as a plumbing, drainage, irrigation and borewell line for building services and water infrastructure, not as process piping. It will not appear in NORSOK, NACE, offshore or oil & gas contexts, and CRA-focused pages are untouched.

## Technical detail

Most of the plumbing survived the earlier removal: `products.types.ts` still carries the `pvc-piping` category and its metadata, `types.ts` still has the `polymer` family label and slug, `matrix.ts` still maps polymer grades to PVC product slugs, `cad.ts` still holds the 1.4 g/cm³ polymer density, and `equivalents.ts` still has UPVC / CPVC / SWR rows. Two data files need recreating and a handful of lists need the entries added back.

New files:
- `src/data/products.pvc.ts` — five `ProductType` records (`upvc-pressure-pipes`, `borewell-column-pipes`, `cpvc-pipes`, `swr-drainage-pipes`, `pvc-fittings`), slugs matching the existing `polymerProductGrades` map in `matrix.ts` and the images in `src/assets/products/`.
- `src/data/materials.polymer.ts` — three `Material` records with slugs `upvc`, `cpvc`, `swr-pvc` (matching `matrix.ts` and `equivalents.ts`), using the same schema as the metal families with polymer-appropriate values (pressure class / SDR instead of pressure-temperature ratings, no UNS — a dash placeholder, density 1.4–1.55 g/cm³).

Edits:
- `src/data/products.ts` — import and spread `pvcProducts`.
- `src/data/products.types.ts` — add `pvc-piping` to `categoryOrder` (last position).
- `src/data/materials.ts` — spread `polymerMaterials` and append `polymer` to `families`.
- `src/components/site/GradeFinder.tsx` — add a `pvc` product form mapped to the `pvc-piping` category with IS-4985 class / SDR sizing.
- `src/data/landed.ts` — restore the PVC commodity row.
- `src/routes/tools.tsx` — restore the UPVC entry in the density list.
- `src/data/shipping.ts` — restore the "UPVC / CPVC / SWR piping systems" scope row.
- Head metadata for the new category and product routes is generated by the existing dynamic `head()` logic — no route files need adding.

Verification: `bunx tsgo`, a build, and a Playwright pass over `/products`, one PVC product page, `/materials` and `/tools`.
