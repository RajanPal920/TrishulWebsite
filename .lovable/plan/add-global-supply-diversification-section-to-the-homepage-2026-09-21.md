# Add global supply-diversification section to the homepage

## Goal
Add a new homepage section immediately after **Global supply** and before **Buyer platform**, presenting Trishul as a documented, responsive supply partner for global buyers diversifying their sourcing base. Keep the message qualitative and evergreen.

## Homepage section
- Eyebrow: **Supply-chain diversification**
- Title: **A Reliable Partner as Global Buyers Diversify Their Supply Base**
- Opening copy will cover the complete range: nickel alloys, duplex, super duplex, stainless steel, carbon steel, and PVC piping manufactured by sister concern Apex Polymer & Engineering. It will not frame Trishul as a flanges-only supplier.
- Use the existing graphite/gold design system, with a full-width section treatment that fits the homepage’s alternating rhythm and avoids another uniform card grid.

## Content structure
1. **Credential badge row**
   - ISO 9001:2015
   - Importer-Exporter Code (IEC)
   - GST registered
   - MSME / Udyam registered
   - DPIIT Startup India recognised
   - CIFFA-trained export desk
   - Present these as compact, accessible badges using existing typography, borders, and gold accents.

2. **Three buyer-confidence columns**
   - **Documentation transparency:** sample MTC available before ordering; heat-number-traceable EN 10204 documentation; third-party inspection available for every consignment when specified.
   - **Commercial ease:** English-language business communication and standard Incoterms—FOB, CFR, CIF, and DDP.
   - **Responsive RFQs:** reuse the existing verified **< 6 h median RFQ response** statistic and its working-hours qualification.

3. **Careful claim language**
   - Use the approved accurate wording: **“CIFFA-trained export desk”**, not “CIFFA-affiliated.”
   - Use **“TPI available for every consignment when specified”**, not a blanket claim that every consignment is witnessed.
   - Do not include tariff percentages, anti-dumping comparisons, country cost comparisons, or other volatile numeric trade claims.

## Scope and reuse
- Reuse the existing credentials and RFQ-response data rather than duplicating factual values in the page.
- Keep every existing homepage section, link, image treatment, animation, SEO entry, GradeFinder, bento, and RFQ flow unchanged.
- Do not modify team, employee, or staff content.
